FAQ

Pricing Questions

Are these fixed prices?

No — they're starting points. Every project is quoted individually after I understand the scope. A simple landing page can be $299. A custom SaaS tool with AI features and an admin dashboard will be more.

What's included in every project?

Strategy, build, mobile responsiveness, performance optimization, launch support, and a post-launch support window (7–30 days depending on plan).

Do you take deposits?

Yes — 50% upfront to start, 50% on delivery. For larger projects I split into milestones.

Can I pay in installments?

For projects over $1,000, yes — usually 3 milestones (design, build, launch).

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes. Monthly maintenance and monitoring plans are available and quoted separately based on your site's complexity.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, Wise, and PayPal. For Pakistani clients, local bank transfer and Easypaisa / JazzCash work too.
